Privacy

Last updated October 18, 2026

BwithU lets you talk face to face with an AI companion. This page explains, in plain words, what happens to your data.

What we process

  • Your voice during a call. It is streamed to our AI provider, Alibaba Cloud Model Studio (Singapore region), so the companion can understand you and answer in real time. We do not record or store your audio.
  • The companion's name, your first name (optional), and the face, vibe and voice you pick. These stay in your browser and are sent with each call so the companion can address you.
  • A random browser ID, your IP address and call length. We use these only to apply the free daily minutes and to stop abuse. Usage counts reset every day.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your data or use it for advertising.
  • We don't keep transcripts of your calls.
  • We don't use your camera.

Our AI provider

Alibaba Cloud processes call audio under its own terms to generate replies. See the Alibaba Cloud privacy policy.

Your choices

You can end a call at any time. Clearing your browser's site data removes everything we stored on your device. For any question or request, email hello@bwithu.live.

Age

BwithU is for people 18 and older.
