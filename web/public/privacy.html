Privacy

Last updated October 25, 2026

BwithU lets you talk face to face with an AI companion. This page explains, in plain words, what happens to your data.

What we process

  • Your voice during a call. When you finish speaking, that part of your audio is sent to Groq, which turns it into text, and the text is used to write the companion's reply. If Groq is unavailable, the same step runs on our own server instead. We do not record or store your audio or the text of what you said.
  • The companion's replies. The text of each reply is sent to Microsoft's speech service to turn it into the voice you hear.
  • The companion's name, your first name (optional), and the face, vibe and voice you pick. These stay in your browser and are sent with each call so the companion can address you.
  • Feedback you choose to send after a call (a rating and optional comment, plus the face, vibe and voice you used). We use it to improve BwithU.
  • A random browser ID, your IP address and call length. We use these only to apply the free daily minutes and to stop abuse. Usage counts reset every day.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your data or use it for advertising.
  • We don't keep transcripts of your calls.
  • We don't use your camera.

Who processes your data

  • Groq (United States) turns your speech into text and generates the companion's replies. See the Groq privacy policy.
  • Microsoft turns the companion's replies into speech. See the Microsoft privacy statement.
  • Oracle Cloud (Mumbai, India) hosts the BwithU server, and Cloudflare carries traffic to it securely.

Each of them processes data under its own terms, only to provide that part of the service.

Your choices

You can end a call at any time. Clearing your browser's site data removes everything we stored on your device. For any question or request, email hello@bwithu.live.

Age

BwithU is for people 18 and older.
